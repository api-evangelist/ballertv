To celebrate the partnership, BallerTV will be giving free premium access to college coaches until youth sporting events resume. BallerTV, the industry leader in youth sports live streaming and on-demand video will be partnering with FastModel, developer of basketball’s premier coaching software products. Athletes and their families will benefit in increased exposure and recruiting opportunities for more than 300,000 games stored on BallerTV’s database with an integration from FastModel Sports’ FastRecruit platform.
