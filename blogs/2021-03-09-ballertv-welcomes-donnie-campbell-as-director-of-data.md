With more than 400,000 games and more than a million athletes covered, BallerTV has the largest and widest video content library in the youth sports market. The next challenge for BallerTV is organizing all the games and data in a way that maximizes exposure for athletes to college coaches and recruiters. BallerTV’s mission is to create an easy-to-use, personalized experience for athletes to showcase their games, highlights, and stats, while also becoming a destination for college coaches and recruiters to identify and track prospects.
