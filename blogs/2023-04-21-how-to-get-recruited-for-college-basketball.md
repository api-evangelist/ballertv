With the first NCAA live period for men’s and women’s basketball recruiting this weekend, it’s a critical time for high school student athletes who are hoping to play at the collegiate level. NCAA Live Periods are NCAA sanctioned periods on the calendar for the Spring and Summer when Division 1 and Division 2 coaches can see you play in person. They are some of the best times to be evaluated, recruited, and offered a scholarship outside of the high school season.
