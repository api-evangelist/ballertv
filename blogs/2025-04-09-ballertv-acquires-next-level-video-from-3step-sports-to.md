LOS ANGELES, CA — April 9, 2025— BallerTV , the premier live-streaming and game film platform transforming youth sports, today announced its acquisition of Next Level Video (NLV) from 3STEP Sports. NLV, a leading sports production company specializing in high-quality game film for club lacrosse and other outdoor sports, joins BallerTV’s growing portfolio. This strategic move accelerates BallerTV’s mission to become the ultimate destination for youth sports coverage — enhancing support for athletes, families, and communities nationwide.
